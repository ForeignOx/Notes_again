A link can essentially be thought of as a bundle of knots tied together
## Definition
A link represents a disjoint union of a set of such embeddings of [[Knots]]
Here disjoint means there are no points which are in the image of two curves
A more precise definition: Fix $n\geq 1$ suppose that $f_{i}:\mathbb{S}^{1}\to \mathbb{R}^{3}$ is an [[Injective Functions|injective]] [[Smooth Functions|smooth]] for each $1\leq i\leq n$ so that $f_{i}(\mathbb{S}^{1})\cap f_{j}(\mathbb{S}^{1})=\emptyset$ for each $i\neq j$, then we call the image
$$
f_{1}(\mathbb{S}^{1})\cup f_{2}( \mathbb{S}^{1} )\cup\dots \cup f_{n}(\mathbb{S}^{1} )\subset \mathbb{R}^{3}
$$## Example
