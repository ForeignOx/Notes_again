Indeed we put an equivalence relation on arcs as follows: we say that two arcs $I$, $I'$ in a link diagram are [[Equivalence Relation|equivalent]] if there is a finite sequence of arcs $I_{1},\dots,I_{n}$ in the diagram such that $I_{1}=I$ and $I_{n}=I'$, and for each $i\in\left\{ 1,\dots,n-1 \right\}$, the arcs $I_{i}$ and $I_{i+1}$ are underpasses of a common crossing
