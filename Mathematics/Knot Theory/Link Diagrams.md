## Definition
A link diagram is a union of [[Knot Diagrams|knot diagrams]], it is a figure $D$ comprised of a finite number of smooth arcs and crossings in $\mathbb{R}^{2}$, such that each arc is either a circle, or begins and ends at a crossing. Locally, at each crossing, exactly three arcs meet, one being the overpass and the other two are underpasses

## Remark
Note that by aying that locally, at each crossing three arcs meet, we ask for each crossing to locally look as in the above picture while we still allow for the possibility that the two underpasses actually belong to one and the same arc
___
Given a link diagram, one can construct a link by embedding the plane $\mathbb{R}^{2}$ via $(x,y)\mapsto(x,y,0)$ and connecting the two underpasses at each crossing by a path whose $z$-coordinate is negative except for its endpoints. This way we see that a link diagram represents an embedding of circles into $\mathbb{R}^{3}$
