## Definition
A link diagram is a union of [[Knot Diagrams|knot diagrams]], it is a figure $D$ comprised of a finite number of smooth arcs and crossings in $\mathbb{R}^{2}$, such that each arc is either a circle, or begins and ends at a crossing. Locally, at each crossing, exactly three arcs meet, one being the overpass and the other two are underpasses
