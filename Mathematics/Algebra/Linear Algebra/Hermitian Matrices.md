## Definition
A matrix $A$ is hermitian if $A^{\dagger}=(A^{*})^{\top}=A$
## Theorem
Let $\hat{A}^{\dagger}=\hat{A}$ be a Hermitian operator, then:
- The eigenvalues are real
- The eigenvectors with different eigenvalues are orthogonal
- There exists an orthonormal basis of $\mathcal{H}$ build from the eigenvectors of $\hat{A}$
