## Theorem
Let $\underline{\alpha}:[a,b]\to \mathbb{R}^{2}$ be a [[Smooth Functions|smooth]] regular simple closed [[Plane Curves|plane]] [[Curves|curve]] of [[arclength|length]] $L=L(\underline{\alpha})$ and $A$ be the area of the domain enclosed by $\underline{\alpha}$, then we have
$$
L^{2}\geq 4\pi A
$$
With equality iff $\underline{\alpha}$ describes a circle
