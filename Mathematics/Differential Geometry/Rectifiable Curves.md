Let $\underline{\alpha}:I\to \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be a [[Curves|curve]] and $[a,b]\subset I$, we say that $\underline{\alpha}$ is rectifiable on $[a,b]$ if the following [[Supremum and Infimum|supremum]] is finite:
$$
L(\underline{\alpha}|_{[a,b]})=\sup \left\{ \sum_{i=0}^{m-1}\lvert \lvert \underline{\alpha}(u_{i+1})-\underline{\alpha}(u_{i}) \rvert \rvert :\middle|: m\in \mathbb{N}\text{ and }a=u_{0}<u_{1}<\dots<u_{m}=b \right\}
$$
In this case we say that $L(\alpha|_{[a,b]})$ is the [[arclength|arclength]] of $\underline{\alpha}:[a,b]\to \mathbb{R}^{n}$
