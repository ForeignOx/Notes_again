## Definition
The projection of a knot onto a plane is called a [[Knots|knot]] or [[link|link]] diagram
A particular knot does not in general have a unique knot diagram as the choice of projection will alter representation produced. Furthermoreif we deform the knot, without letting the curve pass through itself, it will in general also deform the knot diagram
Points with the same $xy$ coordinates but differing $z$ coordinates are drawn crossing over or under each other, with the higher $z$ point depicted as crossing over the lower one
