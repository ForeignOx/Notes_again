## Definition
Take a circle of radius $\varepsilon$ centred on some [[Spacecurves|spacecurve]] $\underline{x}(t)$ perpendicular to the [[Tantrix Curves|tantrix]] $\underline{\hat{T}}(t)$. If there is such a circle for each $t$ and $\varepsilon$ sufficiently small, a tube centred on $\underline{x}(t)$ is obtained, which won't intersect itself. If some curve $\underline{y}$ lies on the tube's surface, it can be used as the base of a surface parametrisation $(t,\phi)$, where $\phi=0$ is taken to be $\underline{y}$
If we define a generating vector $\underline{w}$ which is perpendicular to both $\underline{\hat{T}}$ and $\underline{v}$ (where $\underline{v}$ is as in the definition of a [[ribbons|ribbon]], so normal to $\underline{\hat{T}}$), then the surfave parametrisation can be given as:
$$
\underline{y}(t,\phi)=\underline{x}(t)+\varepsilon(\cos(\phi \underline{v}(t))+\sin(\phi \underline{w}(t)))
$$
The choice of $\underline{y}$ is not unique and is determined by choice of $\underline{v}$. By altering $\underline{y}(t)$, we alter the basis of our surface
