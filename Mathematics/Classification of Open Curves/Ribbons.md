The ribbon is a mathematical construction used to represent physical systems such as the strands of a DNA molecule. 
## Definition
Consider two [[spacecurves|spacecurves]] $\underline{x}(t)$ and $\underline{y}(t)$, such that $\underline{y}(t)=\underline{x}(t)+\varepsilon \underline{v}(t)$, where $\underline{v}(t)$ is a vector normal to $\underline{\hat{T}}(t)$ for $t\in[a,b]$ and $\varepsilon\ll 1$
If $\varepsilon$ is sufficiently small, we can assume that $\underline{y}$ is disjoint from $\underline{x}$. Such a construction will be denoted $R(\underline{x},\underline{v})(t)$, called a ribbon. In choosing $t=s$, it must be noted that $s$ is the arclength of $\underline{x}$, not $\underline{y}$
A closed ribbon requires $\underline{y}$ to be closed over the same period as $\underline{x}$
