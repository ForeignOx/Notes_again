## Definition
Two closed [[spacecurves|spacecurves]] $\underline{x}_{1}$ and $\underline{x}_{2}$ (or [[ribbons|ribbons]] $R_{1},R_{2}$) which can be mapped between each other by a continuous set of homeomorphisms such that the process is reversible are said to share an isotopy. This condition states that we must be able to deform $\underline{x}_{1}$ to $\underline{x}_{2}$ without any self-crossings for [[knots|knots]] and intercrossings for [[Links|links]]
