## Definition
The tantrix curve $\underline{\hat{T}}_{\underline{x}}(t)$ is the curve mapped out by the unit tangent of a [[Spacecurves|spacecurve]] $\underline{x}$, defined as
$$
\underline{\hat{T}}_{\underline{x}}(t)=\frac{\underline{x}'(t)}{\left| \underline{x}'(t) \right| }
$$
The term tantrix is an abbreviation of the more formal Tangent indicatrix
___
One can view the tantrix curve as lying on the surface of the unit sphere. Each vallue of the tantrix curve $\underline{\hat{T}}_{\underline{x}}(s)$ can be represented as a point on the sphere's surface, indicated by an arrow drawn from the centre of the sphere to its surface

The tantrix curve will thus represent a curve lying on the surface of the unit sphere for $s\in[0,L]$. Any continuous transformations applied to curves will cause $\underline{\hat{T}}_{\underline{x}}$ to deform continuously also
