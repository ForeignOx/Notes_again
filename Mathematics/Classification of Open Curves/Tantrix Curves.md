## Definition
The tantrix curve $\underline{\hat{T}}_{\underline{x}}(t)$ is the curve mapped out by the unit tangent of a [[Spacecurves|spacecurve]] $\underline{x}$, defined as
$$
\underline{\hat{T}}_{\underline{x}}(t)=\frac{\underline{x}'(t)}{\left| \underline{x}'(t) \right| }
$$
The term tantrix is an abbreviation of the more formal Tangent indicatrix
