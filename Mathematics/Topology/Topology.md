## Definition
Let $X$ be a [[Sets|set]], a topology on $X$ is a [[Subsets|subset]] $\mathcal{T}$ of the [[Power Sets|power set]] $2^{X}=\left\{ A\subseteq X \right\}$ which satisfy the following:
- $\emptyset$ and $X$ are in $\mathcal{T}$
- Arbitrary unions of elements in $\mathcal{T}$ is in $\mathcal{T}$; if $U_{i}\in \mathcal{T}$ for all $i\in I$, then $\bigcup_{i\in I}U$ 
- Finite intersections of elements in $\mathcal{T}$ is in $\mathcal{T}$
