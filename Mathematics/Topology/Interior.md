## Definition
Let $X$ be a [[topology|topological space]] and $A\subseteq X$, the interior of $A$ is the largest [[Open Sets|open set]] contained in $A$, we enote this $A^{0}$, so $A^{0}\subseteq A$
___
Consider $O_{A}$ the set of all open set contained in $A$, note $\emptyset\in O_{A}$, then
$$
A^{0}=\bigcup_{U\in O_{A}}U
$$
Which is open as it is a union of open sets
