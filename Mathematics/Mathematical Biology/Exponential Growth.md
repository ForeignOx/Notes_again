## Example
Consider a population of greenflies on Adam's chives $x(t)\in[0,\infty)$, for a first model let's say that they have infinite food and no-one dies :)
How does the population size change in time?
$$
\frac{d x}{dt} =ax
$$
So $\frac{d x}{dt}$ represents the number of greenflies born per year, is proportional to the number of greenflies, so $x=Ae^{ at }$, so $a$ is our growth parameter, $A=x(0)$ is our initial population, and it grows without bound
