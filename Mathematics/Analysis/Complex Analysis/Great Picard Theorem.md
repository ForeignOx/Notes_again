## Theorem
Suppose [[Complex Functions|$f$]] has an [[Isolated Singularity|essential singularity]] at $z=a$, then there exists a (single) point $b\in \mathbb{C}$ such for any punctured ball $B^{*}_{r}(a)$ with $r>0$, on which $f$ is holomorphic, we have
$$
f(B_{r}^{*}(a))\supset  \mathbb{C}\setminus \left\{ b \right\}
$$
