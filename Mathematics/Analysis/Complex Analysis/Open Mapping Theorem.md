## Theorem
If $U$ is open and $f$ is holomorphic, then $f(U)$ is open
## Holomorphic Inverse Mapping Theorem
Let $D$ and $D'$ be domains and suppose $f:D\to D'$ is a bijection, then the inverse $f^{-1}:D'\to D$ is holomorphic on $D'$ 
## Riemann Mapping Theorem
