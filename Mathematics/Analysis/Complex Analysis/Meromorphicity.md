## Definition
Let $D$ be a [[Domains|domain]] and let $S\subset D$ be a discrete [[sets|set]], then a function [[Complex Functions|$f$]] is meromorphic on $D$ if it is [[holomorphicity|holomorphic]] on $D\setminus S$ and has [[poles|poles]] at all points in $S$
## Definition
Given a simple closed contour $\gamma$, we say $f$ is meromorphic on and inside $\gamma$ if there exists a domain $D\supset D_{\gamma}\cup\gamma$ on which $f$ is meromorphic
