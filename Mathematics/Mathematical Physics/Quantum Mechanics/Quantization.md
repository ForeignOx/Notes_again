## The Experiment displaying quantization
Various quantities are observed to be quantized such as [[Photoelectric Effect|the photoelectric effect]], [[Black Body Spectra|black body radiation]], and in general [[Energy|energy]], which means they only appear in discrete values. 
Max Planck said that energy can only appear in multiples of the frequency:
$$
E=hf
$$
Where $h$ is Planck's constant
The [[History of the atom#1913 Niels Bohr|Bohr's model of the atom]] showed that electrons have discrete [[Angular Momentum|angular momenta]], 
$$
L=n\hbar
$$
Where $\hbar=\frac{h}{2\pi}$
