## Definition
We define the spacetime distance for special relativaty as
$$
\Delta s ^{2}=(\Delta x)^{2}-c^{2}(\Delta t)^{2}
$$
Which is the quantity that remains the same after a [[Lorentz Boosts|Lorentz boost]]
We have that
$$
\Delta x'=\gamma(\Delta x-v\Delta t)
$$
$$
 \Delta t'=\gamma\left( \Delta t-\frac{v}{c^{2}}\Delta x \right)
$$
Spacetime distance in different cases means:
- $\Delta s^{2}<0$ for events with timelike separation
- $\Delta s^{2}=0$ for events with lightlike separation, we call it this because for this to occur events must be connected by a ray of light
- $\Delta s^{2}>0$ for events with spacelike separation
