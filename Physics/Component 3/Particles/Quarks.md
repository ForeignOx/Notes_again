[[Elementary Particles|Elementary]] particles, not found in isolation, which combines to form [[Hadrons|hadrons]] and [[Baryons|baryons]], e.g. up, down. Evidence for the existence of quarks is indirect since single quarks are never detected
Quarks always have fractional charge, they are fermions with spin $\frac{1}{2}$, they have baryon number $\frac{1}{3}$. Mass varies from a few $\pu{MeV/c^{2}}$ for $u$, $d$ to $174\pu{GeV/c^{2}}$ for $t$
Each quark has an antiquark partner with the same mass and spin, but opposite charge and baryon number
Quarks come in $\hspace{0pt}3$ generations; up and down quarks make up ordinary matter, strange quarks are needed to explain kaons and hyperions and then the third generation is also there ig
