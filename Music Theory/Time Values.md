# Types of Beat
## Crotchet
The sign most often used for a single [[beat|beat]] is: 
```abc
G2
```
And it is called a crotchet
## Minim
A minim lasts as long as two crotchets or beats, and it is symbolised:
```abc
G4
```
## Semibreve
A semibreve lasts as long as two minims or four crotchets and is symbolised:
```abc
G8
```
In america they call these whole notes, then the other notes the corresponding fractions
## Breve
As we might presume from semibreve, there is a sound that lasts twice a long, or $\hspace{0pt}8$ crotchets and it is usually symbolised:
```abc
G16
```
## Quaver
A quaver lasts as long as a half-beat, or half a crotchet and it is symbolised:
```abc
G
```
## Semiquaver
A semiquaver is half a quaver or a quarter of a crotchet, and it is symbolised:
```abc
G1/2
```
## Demisemiquaver
There are also notes half the length of semiquavers, known as semidemiquavers, so an eighth of a crotchet:
```abc
G1/4
```
